What the numbers mean

Spent
How much money has gone into boosting the influencer's posts so far.
Budget
How much we planned to spend.
Impressions
How many times the post appeared on someone's screen. One person can see it more than once.
Clicks
How many times someone tapped the post to visit carsales.
CTR%
Click-through rate: the share of impressions that led to a click. Higher is better.
CPC
Cost per click: spent divided by clicks. Lower is better.
CPM
Cost per 1,000 impressions. Lower is better.
Burst
One run of paid boosting on one platform, with a start and end date. An influencer can have more than one.
